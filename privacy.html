Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

This policy describes the Google account access associated with eBay Gateway Owner API, a privately operated tool used by its account owner.

Data accessed

Google Sheets data may include product research, sourcing records, workflow queues, processing status, reviews and audit receipts. The Apps Script application currently also requests script-management and Gmail modification permissions. Staging Gmail utilities, when explicitly invoked, may search matching email messages and apply or change Gmail labels. The ordinary production Gateway triggers operate on Google Sheets and do not run the staging Gmail polling function.

Use of data

The operator uses these data to manage cross-border e-commerce research, document workflow outcomes, prepare draft listings and verify processing integrity. This public website does not ask visitors to sign in to Google or submit personal information.

Storage and retention

Operational records are maintained in Google services and spreadsheets under the operator's account and access controls. The tool does not impose a fixed automatic deletion period. The retention of particular records depends on the operator's workflows and Google account settings.

Sharing

Google provides authentication and infrastructure for storing and processing workflow information. The operator's separate commerce workflow can involve marketplace services. This policy does not grant public access to private spreadsheets, emails, tokens or credentials. The website itself publishes no operational records or message contents.

Security and account choices

Google account owners can review or revoke application access through their Google Account security settings. Revocation may interrupt connected workflows. The operator controls access to the Google files used by the tool.

Contact

For privacy or deletion questions, contact ccyuichi@gmail.com.
